Privacy Policy

Last updated: . Read the Terms of Service.

What we collect

To work, Aventra stores Discord IDs for servers, channels, roles, and users, plus the settings you choose. Features you turn on store their own data, such as moderation cases (case number, reason, and duration), log settings, and verification records, including a linked Roblox account where Roblox verification is used. Automod may scan messages to apply your rules.

What we don’t collect

We never sell your data or share it with advertisers.

How long we keep it

Data stays while the bot is in your server. Remove the bot and your server’s data is deleted within 30 days. Members can ask for their own data to be removed.
